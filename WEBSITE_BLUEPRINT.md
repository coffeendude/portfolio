# AndrewBrought.com v2 — implementation blueprint

## Purpose
Transform the site from a creative engineering portfolio into an executive personal platform for a technical founder-operator.

## The visitor should understand quickly
- Andrew is technically deep.
- Andrew works beyond engineering.
- Andrew connects systems, products, customers, revenue, and company strategy.
- His performer background strengthens communication and leadership.
- He is building toward founder and startup-executive paths.

## Homepage sequence

### 1. Hero
**Technical founder-operator**

# I build at the intersection of technology, product, and growth.

I bring platform engineering depth, product judgment, technical GTM, and founder operating experience to complex products and ambitious early-stage companies.

CTAs:
- Explore selected work
- Start a conversation

### 2. Operating range
# Technical depth is only valuable when it creates movement.

**Build the foundation**  
Reliable infrastructure, distributed systems, data platforms, observability, production readiness, and technical architecture.

**Shape the product**  
Customer discovery, product definition, roadmap judgment, implementation strategy, and market translation.

**Create momentum**  
Technical GTM, executive alignment, partnerships, revenue strategy, and founder-level execution.

### 3. Selected work
Prioritize:
- Vigour
- Platform and data systems
- Technical GTM and founder operations
- Carefully labeled current experiments

### 4. Thinking
# Ideas I am actively working through.

Show 3–6 high-signal essays or working theses. Each item should state the central question, not merely the title.

### 5. From stage to systems
Use a restrained split layout with one stage image and one current portrait.

Key idea:
`The stage taught me that every complex outcome is both a system and a story.`

### 6. Contact
# Let’s build something consequential.

Provide clear paths:
- Discuss a role
- Explore a venture
- Build together
- Invite me to speak

## Visual direction
- Remove generic futuristic imagery.
- No autoplay hero video.
- Preserve a faint echo of the existing teal/green identity.
- Use editorial typography, structured whitespace, and real photography.
- Make the experience feel mature, confident, and human.
